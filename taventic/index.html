The opportunity

Taventic delivers the kind of personalized travel planning experience customers value once they try it. The opportunity now is to help more prospective travelers discover, trust, and choose Taventic—while building the processes to learn from every interaction.

Our conversations surfaced four connected priorities: customer acquisition, lead and referral tracking, value-based pricing, and a stronger platform for growth and fundraising.

Where we'll focus
01 / GO TO MARKET

Customer acquisition & positioning

Find the right travelers and give them a compelling reason to choose Taventic.
  • Prioritize target segments and their buying motivations
  • Assess social, referral, outreach, and partner channels
  • Refine messaging, positioning, and acquisition priorities
02 / GROWTH OPERATIONS

Lead management & referral systems

Turn each conversation into useful insight and a clear next step.
  • Set up a practical lead-tracking / CRM framework
  • Standardize prospect feedback and follow-up capture
  • Build referral and partner outreach workflows; explore AI tools
03 / MONETIZATION

Pricing, packaging & conversion

Align what customers pay with the experience they receive.
  • Review the current fee structure and package options
  • Design tier and pricing tests aligned to traveler needs
  • Address trust barriers, objections, and proof points
04 / NEXT-STAGE READINESS

Growth planning & fundraising

Connect near-term execution with longer-term potential.
  • Define goals, simple KPIs, and business model assumptions
  • Review investor narrative and funding preparation needs
  • Explore relevant introductions where appropriate
Our approach: Not just ideas and coaching. BSC will help create the tools, frameworks, and actionable plans that allow Taventic's founders to execute faster and learn what actually drives bookings.
