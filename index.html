Why now

At YoWAD Tech's current size, the answer is not a heavy marketing program or a costly outside agency. The priority is to make limited time and dollars work harder: clearly define the buyers YoWAD should pursue, build credibility with those buyers, and focus sales and marketing activity on opportunities that can materially increase revenue.

The initial geographic emphasis should be Jacksonville, St. Augustine and Northeast Florida, where relationship-building, referrals, local credibility and direct outreach can create an advantage. Digital and social channels should then amplify that positioning beyond the local market rather than operate as disconnected marketing activity.

The objective is not to market everywhere. It is to make YoWAD more visible and credible with the right buyers — and build a repeatable path from visibility to conversations, proposals and larger contracts
